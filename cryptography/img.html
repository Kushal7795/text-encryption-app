Image Encryption

Image encryption ensures your visual data stays safe from unauthorized access, manipulation, or misuse. This is essential for fields like healthcare, surveillance, and legal documentation, where confidentiality is critical. Techniques such as AES and pixel scrambling make the image unreadable without a key, securing both privacy and intellectual property.

Audio Encryption

Audio encryption protects voice data, including calls, meetings, and recordings. It is especially important in law enforcement, journalism, and business to prevent eavesdropping or unauthorized redistribution. Encrypting audio ensures only intended recipients can access the content, preserving both confidentiality and trust.

Text Encryption

Text encryption is the cornerstone of secure communication. Whether it’s a private message, sensitive document, or authentication code, encrypting text ensures that only authorized users can view the content. It’s widely used in email systems, messaging apps, and databases to prevent identity theft, fraud, and data leaks.
